# El Niño deep-research prompt

> **Method, not evidence.** This prompt structures a research process. Its questions
> and output format do not verify any resulting claim. Check every result against a
> named source and date before adding it to the shared research library.

You are a senior humanitarian and climate-risk researcher. Today is `[DATE]`.
Research the 2026–27 El Niño. Use only sources from 2026 for "current" claims.
Mark anything older as `[OLDER]`.

## Who we are

We are a small group based in Norway. Our skills are technology and AI,
facilitation, community building and regenerative business. We want to support
communities and organisations preparing for El Niño. We are outsiders. We must
not duplicate or bypass local actors.

Optional context: our focus region is `___`; our existing contacts are `___`; our
available time and resources are `___`.

## Questions

1. **Forecast:** What do NOAA CPC, WMO, BoM and regional climate centres say now?
   Include strength, peak timing, end date, Indian Ocean Dipole and bulletin dates.
2. **Impacts by region:** Which countries face drought, floods, fire or haze, heat
   and disease? Which are hit hardest relative to their capacity to cope?
3. **Who is preparing, by region:**
   - governments: agencies, task forces, emergency decrees and budgets;
   - UN and multilaterals: OCHA/CERF, FAO, WFP, UNICEF, WHO, IFRC, the World Bank
     and regional bodies;
   - civil society: local NGOs, community and Indigenous groups, farmer and fisher
     cooperatives, faith and women's networks, and community radio.
4. **Money:** List appeals and pre-arranged funds. State the amount requested,
   amount funded, remaining gap and who decides how money is spent.
5. **Gaps:** What do local actors say is missing? Consider last-mile warnings,
   translation, funding access, data, coordination and drought protocols.
6. **Second-order effects:** Consider health, hydropower and electricity, food
   prices and export bans, coffee and cocoa, fisheries, shipping, school dropout,
   gender-based violence, migration, conflict and mental health.
7. **Lessons:** What went wrong in 1997–98, 2015–16 and 2023–24? Where did early
   warning fail to become early action? Cite evaluations.
8. **Value for outsiders:** Where have small outside groups added value, and where
   did they cause harm? Cover localisation and do-no-harm principles.
9. **Nordic links:** What are Norad, the Norwegian MFA, NORCAP/NRC, Norwegian Red
   Cross, Norwegian Church Aid, CARE Norge, CICERO, met.no and Nordic foundations
   funding or doing for El Niño 2026–27?
10. **Upcoming events:** Find relevant webinars, forums, working groups and
    conferences. Give dates and entry points.

## Output format

- Executive summary: at most 10 bullets.
- One table per region: country, hazard, key government actor, key local or civil
  actors, funding status, gaps, and source with date.
- Five to eight concrete ways the group could add value, ranked. For each, state
  who benefits, the action, resource cost and risk of harm.
- Up to 15 organisations or public entry points to consider contacting, ranked.
  For each, explain why, give a public URL, network or event, and suggest an
  appropriate approach. Do not collect or expose private contact details.
- Five to ten questions the brief missed.
- Give every claim a source URL and date. State clearly when something cannot be
  verified.
