Internal reference for the SENRI El Niño work. It records who is in the group, what each person
brings, and what they took on at the first group call (6 November 2026). Profile details are
summarised from each person's public LinkedIn profile as of 7 November 2026. Ask the person
before quoting this outside the group, and send corrections as a pull request.

  adoption by large partners, and at least covering the group's AI costs. Owns sharing El Niño news
  with the group and circulating the strategy draft for edits.
